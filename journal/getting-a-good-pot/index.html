Getting a good pot out of four teas

The most common mistake with loose-leaf tea is water that is too hot. It makes delicate green teas bitter. At the tea room the temperature is written beside every tea on the menu, so you can copy it at home.

Dragon Well likes 75 degrees for 2 minutes. Gyokuro is gentler still, at 60 degrees for 2 minutes. Darjeeling First Flush takes 90 degrees for 3 minutes, and our Earl Grey Classic is happiest at 95 degrees for 4 minutes.

Pour off all of the tea when the time is up, so the leaves do not keep steeping in the pot. The leaves of the better teas will give you a second and even a third cup, and each one tastes a little different.
